1. Overview & Legal Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how The Progress Labs ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the RAIZEN mobile application available on Google Play.

We comply strictly with the Google Play Developer Program Policies, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

3. Data Security & Encryption

We implement multi-layered industry-standard technical safeguards:

  • In Transit: All data transmitted between the RAIZEN app and our cloud servers is encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • At Rest: Cloud records stored in Supabase PostgreSQL databases are safeguarded with enterprise AES-256 encryption.
  • On-Device Sandbox: Offline workout logs and cache are stored in an encrypted, sandboxed local SQLite database accessible only by the RAIZEN application.
  • Strict Access Control: Row-Level Security (RLS) policies enforce that you can only read or write your own verified records.

4. Account Deletion & Right to Erasure (Google Play Mandate)

In accordance with the Google Play User Data Policy and GDPR Article 17, you retain the permanent right to delete your entire account and all associated cloud and local data at any time.

🗑️ How to Permanently Delete Your Data:

Method 1 (Instant In-App Deletion):

Open RAIZEN → Navigate to Profile → Scroll to Account & Data Deletion → Tap Delete Account & Wipe Cloud Data.

Method 2 (Web / Remote Deletion Request):

If you have uninstalled the app, email theprogresslabs.com@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" from your registered email. All cloud records will be permanently purged within 72 hours.

What is erased upon deletion: Your authentication credentials, display profile, physical measurements, workout histories, streak records, meditation logs, XP transaction ledger, and cloud backups.
