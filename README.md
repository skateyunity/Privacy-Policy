# Privacy Policy for RAIZEN (Dawn Corps Edition)

**Last Updated**: August 17, 2026  
**Developer**: The Progress Labs  
**Contact / Support**: theprogresslabs.com@gmail.com  

---

## 1. Overview
**RAIZEN** is an anime-inspired calisthenics training and mindfulness application designed to help users build physical strength, discipline, and endurance through bodyweight fitness. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information in compliance with the **Google Play Developer Program Policies**, the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, and the **California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)**.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information
- **Account Credentials**: Email address and display name / username when you register an account.
- **Authentication Identifiers**: Secure Supabase user identifier (UUID) for account management.

### B. Health & Fitness Data
- **Physical Metrics**: Age, height, weight, and fitness experience level provided during onboarding.
- **Training Logs**: Completed calisthenic workouts, exercises performed, sets, reps, duration, streak counts, and earned XP.
- **Mindfulness Data**: Meditation timer minutes and breathing session completions.

### C. Device & App Information
- **Local Settings**: Haptic feedback preferences, sound settings, and notification choices.
- **Crash & Diagnostic Data**: Anonymous error logs if an unexpected app crash occurs.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We use your data solely to:
1. Generate personalized bodyweight training schedules and calisthenic progression recommendations.
2. Track your daily workout streaks, Corps rank progression (Civilian to Hashira), and achievements.
3. Synchronize your progress across your devices when you sign in.
4. Maintain application security, prevent fraudulent multi-accounting, and fix technical bugs.

**We DO NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal or health data to advertisers or third-party data brokers.**

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security
- **In Transit**: All communication between RAIZEN app and our cloud servers (Supabase) is protected with TLS/HTTPS encryption.
- **At Rest**: Cloud databases are hosted on enterprise infrastructure with AES-256 encryption.
- **Local Storage**: Workout logs and offline progress are stored in a private, sandboxed SQLite database on your device.

## 6. User Rights & Account Deletion (Google Play / GDPR / CCPA)

You have full control over your personal data:
- **In-App Account & Data Deletion**: You can permanently delete your account, cloud profile, and all associated training records instantly inside the app by navigating to **Profile > Account & Data Deletion > Delete Account & Wipe Cloud Data**.
- **Web Account Deletion Request**: If you have uninstalled the application, you can submit an account deletion request by emailing **theprogresslabs.com@gmail.com** with your registered email address. We will permanently purge all your cloud records within 72 hours.
- **Data Export**: You can export a full copy of your training data in JSON format at any time via **Profile > Export Fitness Data**.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
RAIZEN is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete it from our systems.
